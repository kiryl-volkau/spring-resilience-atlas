Method

What a colour means. Every row is one entry point of the application (HTTP endpoint, consumer, scheduled job, event listener, SOAP operation, job). The colour is computed by the report generator from the audit records; no model writes a colour into this page.

  • RED · Broken a verified finding of CRITICAL or HIGH severity (or an unverified one with HIGH confidence): acknowledged work can be lost, duplicated or corrupted. Needs work.
  • YELLOW · Smells something fragile that works today (lower severity, lower confidence, or a mitigation outside this repository), or a triage suspicion that still waits for a deep audit.
  • GREEN · OK analysed and no open finding. At the TRIAGE tier this means "no risk signal in the entry method and one hop", not "proven safe". At DEEP/VERIFIED it means a flow was traced with file:line evidence and the audit rules found nothing.
  • GREY · Not analysed no verdict, or the verdict is stale because its files changed. The static hint column shows what the $0 pre-classification suspects; it never becomes a colour by itself.

Tiers. STATIC: deterministic scan and classification by the jar (every operation, $0). TRIAGE: a cheap model reads the entry method and one hop per batch of operations in one domain. DEEP: a flow is traced into an evidence file and audited rule by rule. VERIFIED: an independent agent in a fresh context tried to disprove each RED finding. Confidence is the audit's own estimate; any mitigation marked NOT_CHECKED caps it at MEDIUM.

Evidence. Each finding cites file:line with a verbatim snippet. The jar re-reads those lines at the pinned commit (verify-evidence) and rejects mismatches, so no cited line is invented.

Coverage. "No findings" is never "safe". The ledger above lists what ran, on what, for how much, and what did not run. Operations the plan skipped stay GREY.
